## 📖 What is NobinKrishi?

**NobinKrishi** means "New Agriculture" in Bangla. It's a smart farming platform that uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help farmers grow better crops, earn more money, and connect directly with people who want to buy their produce.

Think of it as a **helpful farming assistant** that lives on your smartphone, available 24/7 to answer questions, give advice, and connect you with buyers.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

**To make farming easier, more profitable, and sustainable by bringing modern technology to every farmer in Bangladesh and other countries.**

We believe every farmer deserves:
- ✅ Fair prices for their hard work
- ✅ Access to expert knowledge
- ✅ Tools to grow healthier crops
- ✅ Direct connections to buyers (no middlemen!)
- ✅ Protection from crop diseases and weather problems
